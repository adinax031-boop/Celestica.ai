05 / Scientific results
Measure, don't assume.
Lower training loss did not translate into higher held-out accuracy. That is the central finding of this pilot.

Decision: keep the original model for general Rust tasks; retain the experimental adapter for further research. Repair improved from 3/9 to 4/9, but generation declined from 9/21 to 2/21.
What we contributed
The project designed the research question, AI-assisted Rust examples, independent tests, evaluation protocol and comparative analysis. Qwen, LoRA, Rust and WASI are existing technologies, not inventions of Celestica.
What remains unknown
Single seed, small synthetic training set, short functions, potential public-benchmark pretraining overlap. Tests establish behavior only on tested cases. No frontier-model transfer or general superiority claim is supported yet.
